I work on products that turn everyday communication into useful actions—from business workflows in WhatsApp to connecting with people nearby.

Selected projects below show what I’m building, the problems they address and their current stage.

## BotMaker / WaPa

A platform for creating and managing business workflows through WhatsApp. Business owners can describe a process, try a private draft, request changes, and confirm publication. Controls include pause/resume and returning to a previous release.

The broader platform and project family includes:

- **Budget Planner:** conversational budgeting and financial-document workflows.
- **Service operations:** requests, follow-up, and handoff to the business owner.
- **Venue booking:** shared-space booking and approval workflows.
- **Salon appointments:** appointment requests, rescheduling, cancellations, and owner approval.
- **Marketing and reporting:** content workflows and business reporting.

[Project overview](https://github.com/Rippleaitech/botmaker) · [Interactive website demos](https://botmaker-ripple.netlify.app/)
